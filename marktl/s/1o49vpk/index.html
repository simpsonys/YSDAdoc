YSDAWAY / Vision Review / Include Myself

Not a race, but my journey.

This note reads as a personal operating manifesto for 2030: a shift from exhaustion to chosen contribution, from dependence to freedom, and from self-erasure to deliberate self-inclusion. The core tension is not ambition versus care, but whether ambition can be rebuilt around health, presence, and sovereignty.

Review Lens

Review this as both a life direction statement and a decision filter. The strongest passages clarify identity, boundaries, and long-term health. The main review question is whether the document is concrete enough to guide choices without losing its emotional force.

Presentation Summary

The note has strong emotional authority and a coherent philosophical spine. It feels most premium when treated as a sequence of carefully paced declarations: identity, work, health, freedom, family, boundaries, and final choice.

Review focus Ask whether this document should remain a manifesto, or whether it now deserves one more layer: operating principles that can be checked monthly and defended during periods of stress.
01 Identity Direction

The future self is named before it is engineered.

The opening establishes a target state, then reaffirms it in Korean with a more embodied cadence. This section gives the document its emotional authority and sets the tone for every later claim.

Anchor
“By 2030, I am healthy, free, and creatively alive.”
나는 건강하고 자유롭게, 내가 원하는 곳에서, 내가 원하는 시간에, 내가 원하는 일을 하며 살아간다.
Interpretation

This is not a productivity goal. It is a sovereignty statement. “Healthy,” “free,” and “creatively alive” are not parallel aspirations here; they operate like minimum conditions for a life that feels fully inhabited.

Finding

The opening is memorable, portable, and emotionally legible. It works as a title, a mantra, and a test.

Risk

The phrase “free” is powerful but broad. In later sections it gains meaning, but here it still needs operational edges.

Opportunity

The bilingual rhythm can be treated as a formal design feature: English for thesis, Korean for embodiment and conviction.

Section Review Prompt What should a reviewer test here?
Finding

This section successfully names the desired identity before listing tactics or obligations.

Open Question

Does “creatively alive” need one concrete picture so it can survive high-pressure seasons?

Reader Note

Mark whether the opening feels inspiring enough to revisit, not just accurate enough to agree with.

02 Creation Work

Work is reclaimed as expression, not depletion.

This section shifts the note from aspiration into authorship. It names making as the place where identity returns, while also placing a boundary between value creation and self-consumption.

Core Claim
“I create because creation brings me back to myself.”
나는 무언가를 만들 때 가장 나답다.
Expansion

Software, writing, education, tools, stories: the list is broad on purpose. The note resists premature narrowing and instead defines a deeper continuity, which is that the work carries mind, experience, and care.

Boundary

“My work gives value to others, but it no longer consumes me.” This is one of the clearest anti-burnout corrections in the entire document. It reframes contribution as chosen strength rather than enforced sacrifice.

Section Review Prompt Review the work philosophy and pressure boundaries
Finding

The note convincingly separates meaningful work from self-erasing overwork.

Open Question
  • What kinds of creation are non-negotiable?
  • What work patterns would count as “consuming me” again?
Reader Note

This section may be strong enough to become a standalone career principle or homepage statement.

03 Health Foundation

Health is not support material. It is the structural base.

The emotional center of gravity deepens here. Health is described in physical, practical, and relational terms, and the fear of repeating an old pattern makes the section feel earned rather than abstract.

Foundation

“My health is the foundation of everything.” This line is direct, and the next sentence proves it through specifics: body, sleep, blood pressure, metabolism, peace of mind.

Consequence

The statement about not leaving pain behind for loved ones turns health from self-maintenance into responsibility with boundaries. It avoids vanity and centers stewardship.

Identity
“I create with strength, not exhaustion.”
나는 건강하게 오래 곁에 남는 사람이 된다.
Section Review Prompt Check for credibility, clarity, and future accountability
Finding

This is one of the strongest and most credible sections because it names concrete health dimensions, not just ideals.

Open Question

Would this become more durable if it named 2 to 4 measurable health commitments that define “protected” health?

Reader Note

If any line should shape calendar design, work limits, and travel decisions, it is this section.

04 Freedom Sovereignty

Freedom becomes believable when identity and money are uncoupled.

This section moves from emotional language into strategic posture. Company, role, country, schedule, and identity are all treated as containers that may exist, but must not become prisons.

Freedom

“My freedom is real.” The note immediately defines what that means by listing forms of entrapment that are no longer acceptable: one company, one role, one country, one schedule, one identity.

Company
“My company is a source of income and optionality, not my identity.”
나는 회사에 소속되어 있을 수는 있지만, 회사에 얽매이지 않는다.
Wealth

The 50 billion won ambition is framed not as fantasy, but as disciplined long-term execution. The note explicitly rejects luck and centers judgment, patience, and sustainable creation.

Section Review Prompt Review ambition, realism, and decision usefulness
Finding

The note gives financial ambition moral and strategic context instead of presenting it as status-seeking.

Open Question
  • What would count as optionality in practice by 2027 or 2028?
  • How should “not my identity” shape work choices today?
Reader Note

The financial target is bold. A reviewer may want to know whether the number is symbolic, strategic, or specifically modeled.

05 Family Boundaries

Presence replaces sacrifice as the model of care.

The family passages are not only affectionate. They are corrective. They reject the identity of the endlessly responsible father and replace it with a healthier model: steady, bounded, present, and alive.

Children

The children do not merely need inheritance or output. They need example, steadiness, and a father who does not disappear into duty. This is one of the note’s clearest redefinitions of love.

Boundaries

“I am no longer the unlimited guarantor...” is a major threshold line. It does not reject care; it rejects limitlessness. That distinction is psychologically and structurally important.

Correction
“I can support without surrendering.”
나는 누구도 버리는 것이 아니다. 나는 내가 베풀어 온 돌봄 안에 나 자신도 포함시킨다.
Section Review Prompt Review emotional clarity and boundary integrity
Finding

This section contains the most mature boundary language in the document. It preserves compassion while rejecting martyrdom.

Open Question

What situations still trigger the “unlimited guarantor” pattern, and how will this manifesto interrupt them?

Reader Note

The final Korean lines are among the strongest in the note. They may deserve visual emphasis or repetition elsewhere.

06 Closing Verdict

The ending works because it becomes choice, not wish.

The closing sequence strips away passivity. It rejects permission and luck, then lands on a repeated act of choosing. This gives the note a clean final rhythm and a usable voice.

Turn

“I am not waiting for permission. I am not waiting for luck.” This is the hinge where vision becomes agency.

Identity

“I am becoming the person who can live that life.” The line avoids magical thinking. It names personal development, not just desire.

Cadence

The repeated “I choose...” structure gives the note a ceremonial ending without becoming grandiose. “I choose to include myself” is the correct final line because it reframes every earlier promise.

Overall Finding

The manifesto is emotionally coherent, memorable, and strong enough to function as a personal constitution.

Main Gap

It would benefit from a second layer of operational definitions for health, freedom, and boundaries.

Editorial Verdict

Keep the lyricism. Add structure around it rather than reducing its emotional intensity.
